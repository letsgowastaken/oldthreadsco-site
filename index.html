— 02 / The Story

Built on obsession.

Inside the shop · Orem, UT

We started Old Threads Co because we were tired of overpriced "vintage" that wasn't actually vintage. So we built the shop we wanted to walk into.

Every piece on our racks is hand-picked — sourced from estate sales, auctions, and the kind of dusty corners most people walk past. We hunt for the real stuff: single-stitch tees, made-in-USA denim, faded sweats with stories baked in.

And we keep prices fair. Vintage shouldn't be a flex — it should be wearable. Our whole thing is making the good stuff accessible to anyone who cares about how their clothes feel, fit, and last.

Come through the shop, follow the drops, or just say hi. We're a small team and we love this stuff.
