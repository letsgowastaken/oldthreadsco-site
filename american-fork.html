Why American Fork shoppers come down to Orem.

American Fork is one of our most loyal customer cities. Lots of regulars drive down from the AF Main Street area on a Friday to catch the weekly drop. The shop is a quick run down I-15 South.

The shop carries hand-picked vintage tees, denim, jackets, sweatshirts, and one-of-one pieces from the 70s, 80s, 90s, and 2000s. We rotate inventory weekly so the rack is never the same twice. New drops happen every Friday — that's the day to come in if you want first dibs on the rare stuff.

What we carry.

Walk-in customers from American Fork usually find what they're after in one of these categories:

Vintage tees — band tees, college, sports, single-stitch, faded blacks, and rare brand graphics from $15-$60.
Denim — Levi's 501s, Carhartt double-knees, vintage Wrangler. Made-in-USA pairs are usually in stock.
Outerwear — Carhartt Detroit jackets, leather bombers, varsity letterman jackets, vintage Champion crewnecks.
One-of-ones — rare 90s Disney, NBA finals tees, vintage tour merch, and pieces with real history.
Consignment Corner — local Utah collectors bring in their pieces, so you'll find stuff that won't show up anywhere else online.

Getting here from American Fork

From American Fork, take I-15 south to exit 269 (University Pkwy), head east, turn right on 1300 S, then right onto 1350 W. About 13 minutes.
